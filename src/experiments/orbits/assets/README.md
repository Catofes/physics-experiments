Prompt:

Create one production-ready Earth globe sprite for an educational orbital mechanics web application. Square 1024x1024 image. Orthographic full circular globe perfectly centered at (512,512), globe disk diameter exactly 960 pixels (32 pixel margin on all sides). Truly transparent background outside the globe, no stars, no text, no labels, no orbit lines, no cast shadow or outer glow. Style: polished restrained hand-painted scientific illustration, softly shaded sphere, deep teal-blue oceans and natural muted sage-green land with light sand deserts, subtle white cloud wisps that do not hide continents. Recognizable accurate continental silhouettes, Asia centered, Africa on the left and Australia lower right, north at top. Gentle lighting from upper left, subtle darkening at lower right, entire geography readable. Suitable on a dark navy canvas with a clean pale green user interface. Not a photograph, not a flat map, not a cartoon face. Crisp circular silhouette with real alpha transparency. Only Earth.
